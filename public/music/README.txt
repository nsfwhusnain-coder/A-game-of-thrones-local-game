YOUR SOUNDTRACK
===============
The game plays only the music you put here (the old generated score is gone). Supported: mp3, ogg, m4a, aac,
wav, flac, opus, webm. Tracks shuffle within a folder and crossfade when the scene changes.

  music/<house>/   plays while you rule that house. The folder name is the house id, e.g.
                   stark, lannister, baratheon (the crown), baratheon_ds (Stannis), baratheon_se (Renly),
                   targaryen, greyjoy, tyrell, martell, tully, arryn, bolton, frey, manderly,
                   nights_watch, free_folk — any house id works (see public/data/houses.js).
  music/title/     plays on the title screen.
  music/war/       plays while your house is at war (instead of your house folder).
  music/           loose files here play whenever no folder above fits.

The show's soundtrack is copyrighted, so the game does not ship it. If you own it, copy the tracks you like
into the folders — for example the main title into title/, "The Rains of Castamere" into lannister/,
"Goodbye Brother" or "Winter Is Coming" into stark/, "What Is Dead May Never Die" into greyjoy/,
"Mhysa" or "Blood of My Blood" into targaryen/, "The Night's Watch" into nights_watch/.

Files in this folder are not uploaded to GitHub (see .gitignore).
