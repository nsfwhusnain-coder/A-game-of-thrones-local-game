Your own portraits
==================

Drop painted or commissioned portraits here, named by character id, and they replace the
generated ones everywhere in the game:

    eddard_stark.png   tywin_lannister.jpg   daenerys_targaryen.webp

Ids are listed in public/data/characters.js (and in each character's sheet). Portrait
proportions are 5:6 (e.g. 500 x 600). Restart is not needed: reload the page.
Dead characters still use the generated mourning portrait.
