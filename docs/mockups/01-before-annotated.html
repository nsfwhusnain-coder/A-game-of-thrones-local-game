Today: what is wrong

The current UI at turn 0 (docs/screens/e2), before any player input. GDD 17 §1.

  1. 1
    Six-tile resource bar

    Treasury, Levies, Men-at-arms, Ships, Food, Season: 6 labels, 6 values, 6 sub-lines. About 1000 px wide (three quarters of the screen at 1366). The player speaks and the AI acts, so these are read-outs, not levers. Keep 3 vitals.

  2. 2
    Date box + turn counter

    Date, "Turn 0" and "N decisions awaiting" in one box. Drop the turn number; fold the pending count into one Inbox badge.

  3. 3
    Six system icons

    Ravens, music, undo, help, settings, menu: six icons at the same weight. Collapse into one menu button.

  4. 4
    End turn

    The most important control is fine, but its reason ("next turn: 7 days, a quiet week") is a separate tiny line elsewhere. Put it inside the button.

  5. 5
    "Your situation" card

    Five lines of prose plus Aims and Levers, at the top of the feed on turn 0. Show once as a welcome card, then never again.

  6. 6
    "How to play" wall

    Eleven bullets (about 25 lines) that push the real chronicle out of sight. Move to the ? help; replace with three coach marks.

  7. 7
    Order composer in the drawer

    The command bar is trapped in the drawer and dies when the drawer collapses. Give it its own bottom-centre bar.

  8. 8
    Nine map modes

    Realms, Holders, Diplomacy, Knowledge, War, Wealth, Prosperity, Unrest, Terrain behind one dropdown. One dropdown with three pinned favourites is enough.

  9. 9
    Eight-button dock

    Realm, Council, Military, Economy, Diplomacy, Intrigue, People, Chronicle, always on: the "economy and military buttons all the time" the owner objects to, plus a fourth badge system. Replace with three menu entries.

  10. 10
    Portrait plate

    Keep it, it is the player’s face. It is small, sits beside the dock and has no life in it: make it larger, framed and warmer.

  11. 11
    Colliding map labels

    "LANNI~300" and "KINGS SHIPS ING" overlap: army tokens and region names fight. Label declutter is work package U8.
