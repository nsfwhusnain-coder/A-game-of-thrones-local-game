# 13 · Content and data

> What the world must contain for "every great house and every lesser house playable", the data schemas, and the rules
> for sourcing and verifying content. Files: `public/data/*.js`, `data-src/`, `scripts/check-data.js`.

---

## 1. Sourcing rules (normative)

1. **The books first:** *A Song of Ice and Fire* (AGOT → ADWD), then *The World of Ice & Fire* and *Fire & Blood* for
   history, then the Dunk & Egg novellas. **The HBO show only where the books are silent** (e.g. a character's look
   the books never describe), and never for plot.
2. **Nothing after the 1st day of the 8th moon, 298 AC enters a character's knowledge or history** (the existing rule
   in `docs/HANDOFF.md` §7). Future events live only in beats ([10](10-narrative-events.md)).
3. **Placeable or not at all:** a new house needs a seat that exists on (or can be placed on) the Lands of Ice and Fire
   atlas (`data-src/got-inspired-map/`); record the source of the position.
4. **Cite:** every new house/character/holding entry carries a `src` comment (book and chapter, or "World of Ice &
   Fire, <section>"), or `src: 'inferred'` with the reason (e.g. a lord's name the books never give).
5. **Invented people are allowed** for the many unnamed lords' households (castellans, maesters, knights) — generated by
   `generateKin`/`generateLord` with region name tables, flagged `invented: true`, never replacing a canon person.
6. **Accuracy review:** the implementing agent cross-checks names, seats, words and sigils against its knowledge of the
   books; where unsure, it prefers omission to invention and lists the doubt in `docs/HANDOFF.md`.

## 7. Validation (`npm run check`)

`scripts/check-data.js` is extended to fail on:

1. any house without a placeable seat (unless landless by design), without words/sigil/colour, or with an unknown liege;
2. any character without `sex`, `born`, `house`, `nature` scales (for personas), or with a dangling family link;
3. any holding outside the land mask (or at sea without `port`), duplicate positions (< 2 units apart), unknown owner;
4. region population totals outside ±10 % of [06](06-economy.md) §4; musters outside ±10 % of [07](07-military.md) §2.2;
5. any beat whose `names` reference unknown characters, whose windows are out of order within a thread, or whose
   trigger references unknown parties/places;
6. any happening/matter/hook template with an unknown condition key or effect;
7. any text in data containing a phrase from `data/anachronisms.js` (outside beats);
8. id rules for enums: no id that is a strict prefix of another unless they alias the same entity ([04](04-ai-system.md)
   §3.1).
