# The final playtest on Maester-12B (2026-10-02)

*Played by the lead engineer after Phases N–H were merged: the game as it stood on the default branch (CI green) plus the four fixes below, on the owner's model — Maester-12B — through the lead's **own** llama-swap (port 8096, `maester-e2e.yaml`, the owner's server and `config.json` untouched; the GPU was idle, 9.6–9.8 GB in use). The reports are in [`samples/final-playtest-stark.md`](samples/final-playtest-stark.md) and [`samples/final-playtest-blackwood.md`](samples/final-playtest-blackwood.md).*

## How

`WC_CONFIG=<a profile>` with the routing of `deploy/game-routing.json` (`maester-12b` with the adapter and MTP for interpret, mind, director, audience, council, consolidate and the Weaver; `maester-12b:plain` for the narrator and the untested kinds; 64k, thinking off, every slot `null`), `weaver: true`, `logCalls: true`. Then `npm run playtest -- --house stark --turns 12 --seed 7` and `--house blackwood --turns 12 --seed 11` (the script's orders, one audience by raven, one council, the pending matters answered), and `npm run bench -- --suite audience,latency` and `--suite interpret,mind,narrate`.

## What the scenes are like

Good, and period; the facts are kept (a host's numbers, who is where). A sample: *"The dust of the road coated Jory Cassel's throat as he watched the banners of House Stark rise and fall against the summer heat… 'Keep the line tight,' he commanded, his voice dry from the grit."* But formulaic: four of eleven scenes of the Stark game open on the smell or taste of the air ("The air in the Great Hall tasted of…", "reeked of…", "The scent of…") and three close on a trivial watched detail ("He watched a speck of dust land on his sleeve", "a drop of wine hit the rushes"). The council's three counsellors speak in their own offices' words; Luwin once wrote "the Manderleys".

## What it found (all fixed on branch `wp/h6-live-playtest`, D-104)

1. **A card without a fact.** The coherence check at the end of the Stark report: *"Eddard Stark settles the matter of a grievance at court" is backed by no fact.* The card of a matter the lord answered — and of any command the story told nothing of, or that came to nothing — was made by the order's own path and had no fact behind it. The lord's own word is now recorded (`order_given`) and the card names it. (The Stark report in `samples/` is from before the fix, and shows it; the Blackwood report is after.)
2. **A council could be asked for another house's officers.** The script's council (Winterfell's maester, steward and lady) was held in a *Blackwood* game and answered with Winterfell's coin and levies. The interface never offers it, but the server took any name; it now seats only the lord's own people.
3. **The bench could not write into a folder that was not there** (the interpret, mind and narrate suites wrote their report without making it; the first live run lost its result at the last step).
4. **The audience scorer called an amused refusal a yes.** Walder Frey, insulted, dismissed with a laugh; "amused" is a mocking refusal, not warmth. The scorer now counts only "warm" against a refusal. One real miss remains: an insulted own man (Rodrik Cassel), whom the engine settled as *obey*, answered with a refusal in words.

## What it did not exercise

The sound and the read-aloud (no audio device); the Weaver (it was on for twenty-four turns of two games and was offered nothing: no `weaver` call in either log); the browser interface played by a person (the playtest drives the API); a long campaign (twelve turns each); the narrator in `cards` mode.
